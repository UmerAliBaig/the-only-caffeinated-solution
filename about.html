Our Mission

Serving Honest Coffee, One Neighbourhood At A Time

The Only Caffeinated Solution exists to make genuinely good coffee an everyday habit, not an occasional treat — sourced fairly, roasted carefully, and served by people who actually care whether your day gets better.

We also believe a café should feel like a third place: not quite home, not quite the office, but somewhere you're always welcome to stay a while.
