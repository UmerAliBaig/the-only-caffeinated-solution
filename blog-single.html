The Pour-Over Method, Explained Simply

Home / Blog / Pour-Over Method

Pour-over is the closest most of us get to being a barista at home — no machine required, just water, beans and a little patience. Here's how we teach it to every new hire.

What You'll Need

  • A dripper (V60, Chemex or similar) and paper filter
  • Freshly ground coffee, medium-coarse
  • A gooseneck kettle for controlled pouring
  • A kitchen scale, if you have one

The Ratio That Works Every Time

We use a 1:16 coffee-to-water ratio as our house standard. Adjust the total volume for how many cups you're brewing.

CupsCoffee (g)Water (ml)Brew Time

Step By Step

  1. Rinse the filter with hot water to remove papery taste, then discard the water.
  2. Add ground coffee and level the bed.
  3. Bloom with double the coffee weight in water (e.g. 30g water for 15g coffee), wait 30 seconds.
  4. Pour the remaining water in slow concentric circles, avoiding the edges.
  5. Let it fully drain, then serve immediately.
